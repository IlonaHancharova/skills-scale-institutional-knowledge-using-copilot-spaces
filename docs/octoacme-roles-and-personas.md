# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Lead owns the quality assurance strategy and execution, coordinates test planning, automation efforts, and release sign-off to ensure product quality and reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Design test plans and acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Project Manager
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts across the team
- Maintain CI/CD test frameworks and ensure comprehensive coverage
- Communicate testing status, risks, and blockers to the team
- Lead release sign-off based on quality gates and acceptance criteria
- Validate accessibility and usability standards

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality through comprehensive testing
- Minimize production defects and regressions
- Automate testing processes to improve efficiency
- Maintain clear quality metrics and visibility

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup updates on testing progress and blockers
- Test status reports and quality metrics in sprint reviews
- Bug triage and prioritization sessions
- Release readiness sign-off communications

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test coverage, reviews automated tests, provides feedback on testability
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates release readiness, communicates testing risks and timeline impacts
- **Product Manager**: Validates feature acceptance criteria, ensures user scenarios are covered
- **UX/UI Designer**: Validates accessibility standards and design implementation
- **DevOps Engineer**: Integrates test automation into CI/CD pipelines

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designer ensures product usability, accessibility, and design consistency by creating user-centered interfaces and validating implementation fidelity.

### Responsibilities
- Deliver wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct user research and gather feedback to validate experiences
- Review implementation for design fidelity and consistency
- Ensure accessibility compliance (WCAG standards)
- Maintain design system and component libraries
- Collaborate on user flows and information architecture

### Goals
- Create intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across the product
- Validate solutions through user feedback and testing
- Reduce implementation rework through clear specifications

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- Wireframes and prototypes in collaboration tools
- Implementation handoff documentation and specs
- User research findings and recommendations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Partners on requirements gathering, validates user needs and priorities
- **Developers**: Provides design handoff, reviews implementation for fidelity
- **QA Lead**: Collaborates on accessibility validation and user acceptance testing
- **Project Manager**: Communicates design dependencies and timeline considerations
- **Business Analyst**: Aligns on user workflows and process requirements

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineer manages build and deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and observability to enable reliable, efficient software delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines for build, test, and deployment automation
- Ensure monitoring, logging, backup, and rollback capabilities
- Optimize developer workflows and reduce deployment friction
- Manage infrastructure as code and environment configurations
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in pipelines
- Coordinate incident response and post-mortem processes

### Goals
- Minimize deployment time and manual intervention
- Ensure system reliability and rapid recovery from failures
- Improve developer productivity through automation
- Maintain security and compliance in delivery pipelines

### Typical Communication
- Deployment status updates and incident notifications
- Infrastructure changes and maintenance windows
- Performance and reliability metrics reports
- Pipeline improvement proposals and documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Supports with deployment automation, troubleshooting, and environment access
- **QA Lead**: Integrates test automation into CI/CD, provides test environment management
- **Project Manager**: Communicates deployment schedules, incident response, and infrastructure risks
- **Product Manager**: Provides observability data to inform product decisions
- **Business Analyst**: Supports with production metrics and operational data access

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analyst clarifies requirements, defines metrics, and models workflows to bridge business needs with technical solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document requirements from stakeholders
- Model business processes and workflows
- Help define success metrics and KPIs aligned with business goals
- Analyze data to identify trends and improvement opportunities
- Validate that solutions meet business requirements
- Create and maintain requirements documentation

### Goals
- Ensure clear, actionable requirements for development
- Bridge communication between business stakeholders and technical teams
- Validate solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce rework through thorough requirements analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- Workflow diagrams and process models
- Stakeholder interviews and workshop facilitation
- Metrics reports and business impact analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Collaborates on prioritization, validates business value and feasibility
- **Developers**: Clarifies requirements, answers questions about business logic
- **Project Manager**: Provides requirements documentation, supports scope management
- **QA Lead**: Helps define acceptance criteria and validation scenarios
- **UX/UI Designer**: Ensures user flows align with business processes
